Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CloudHub ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use cloudhub.4kun.top (the "Site") and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data required to operate the Site:

  • Account Data: When you sign up, we collect your email address and chosen username. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile info.
  • Usage Data: We track the anime you add to your watchlist and your playback progress so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Technical Data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type) are processed by our infrastructure providers for security and debugging.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to provide the service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to save your session, playback progress, and preferences (like audio/subtitle settings). You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication and database storage, and Google for OAuth login. Their respective privacy policies apply to the data they process on our behalf.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or wish to delete your account, you can do so via the Settings page or by contacting us through the report system on the Site.
